    Backup

    Your data is stored only in this browser on this device. Download a backup now and then so you never lose it. A backup also lets you move your data to another device.

    Rest timer

    The timer starts when you tick off a set. Tap −15 / +15 to change the rest for that exercise – the app remembers it. Default: 2:00 for presses, rows and pull-ups, 1:30 for everything else.

    Install on your phone

    iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
    Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

    Basic-Fit

    Basic-Fit has no public API for personal websites, so visits can't be imported automatically. Every saved workout counts as a visit, and “Check in only” logs a visit without details.
